You can't answer everything, and your customers won't wait.

If you're busy running your business, you don't have time to answer every phone call, SMS, email, social media comment or web form enquiry. You're serving customers, doing the work, managing staff and keeping the whole thing moving. Replying instantly to everyone, all day and half the night, isn't possible.

The problem is that the people sending those messages don't wait. They're interested right now. If they don't hear back quickly, or they don't get the answers they need, they give up and go to whoever does reply. You never find out how many you lost, but it's real money, and a lot of it came from leads you already paid to get.

Then there's the other end of the problem. Getting new people to notice you in the first place keeps getting more expensive. Ads on Meta and social media are crowded and costly, and good ads take time, skill and constant testing that most owners simply don't have.

Advertising

Most businesses advertise in the same few places: Facebook, Instagram and the other social feeds. Everyone is fighting for the same attention, prices keep going up, and your ad is competing with a friend's holiday photos.

We advertise inside apps instead. It's more profitable than Meta and social media, because there's less noise around your ad and people are more likely to take notice and act.

We do all the work. You send us your business information, your website, your pages and socials, and any videos you already have. We turn it into ads that run for 10 to 40 seconds, complete with copy, voiceover, subtitles and a story that fits your market. The only thing we ask you for is who your target audience is.

The story matters more than most people think. People don't act because they read a list of features. They act because something in the ad spoke to them. That's why every ad is built on 20 years of copywriting and an understanding of the psychology behind why people say yes.

Then we launch it, manage it, and keep refreshing the ads so they keep working. You don't have to touch a thing.

AI SMS campaigns

This is a completely separate service from the ads, and it works just as well on its own. Our AI agent talks to your leads and customers by text message for you: bookings, engagement, nurturing, conversion and enquiries.

It's there 24 hours a day, seven days a week. It replies within seconds, answers questions, asks the right questions back, books appointments and follows up with anyone who goes quiet. It sounds natural, friendly and human, never like spam or a robot.

When a conversation needs a real person, say a tricky question, a complaint or a big sale, the agent hands it over to you or your team. You step in only when it counts.

Why SMS? Because text messages have a far higher open rate than email. People read texts, usually within minutes. An email can sit unopened for days.

You don't have to do anything. We set up the agent, tailor it to your business, your services and the way you speak, and keep it running. Meanwhile, you get to focus on the customers in front of you.
